About Workforce Macon

Free data on how Middle Georgia works, and a guide to the region's training and support programs, from the Greater Macon Chamber of Commerce. Updated every quarter.

Workforce Data How Middle Georgia comes to work in Macon The commuting story, then what jobs pay, which industries are growing, and how your county connects to Macon-Bibb. Workforce Navigator Find a program, support, or workers Every training program and support organization in Greater Macon, searchable, with a map of how they connect.

Custom research

Need more than what's here? The Chamber builds custom research for employers, site selectors and partners, such as:

  • Site selection and the labor shed around a site
  • Industry and occupation deep dives
  • Pay benchmarking, from entry-level to experienced, against peer metros
  • Data for grants and program planning

Each report is quoted to fit the question. To start one, call the Chamber at (478) 621-2000 and ask about custom workforce research.

Where the data comes from

Commuting
U.S. Census Bureau, LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES), 2023
Residents
U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey, 2024
Jobs, pay and industries
JobsEQ, based on the Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages and Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics
New manufacturers
Company, Georgia Department of Economic Development and Macon-Bibb County Industrial Authority announcements
Programs and organizations
Each school's and organization's own published listings

Embed the map

Partners can put the live Workforce Navigator map on their own site. It stays up to date on its own.

Preview and tips

The preview is scaled to fit, so full screen won't work inside it; open the map in a new tab to try that.

Preview at:
  • Set a fixed pixel height that fits your layout; the map fills whatever space the frame gives it.
  • Don't put the frame inside another scrolling box. The map already pans and zooms on its own.
  • The map loads its data from this site, so the page it's on must allow embedded frames (most site builders do).
