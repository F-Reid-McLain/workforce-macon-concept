Beyond the Headline Numbers
Macon-Bibb's Adjusted Economic Picture

About 40% of workers employed in Macon-Bibb live outside it. Standard Census metrics follow the worker home — understating the county's income and overstating its neighbors'. The figures below are adjusted for where the work actually happens.

Why the gap exists: About 40% of workers employed in Bibb County reside outside its borders. Residence-based income surveys assign those wages to surrounding counties, suppressing Macon-Bibb's reported income while inflating neighbors' figures — especially Houston County, whose high household-income ranking collapses once workplace-adjusted.
