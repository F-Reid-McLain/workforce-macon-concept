Workforce Macon · from the Greater Macon Chamber of Commerce

Middle Georgia comes to work in Macon.

Every workday, 26,349 people from 16 neighboring counties head into Macon-Bibb for work. Welcome to Workforce Macon, the Chamber's free guide to the people, jobs and industries that make it happen.

The whole picture

The region's commuting balance

Counting commuters from everywhere, not just next door, the pattern holds: every county around Macon-Bibb sends out more workers than it takes in. Only Macon-Bibb gains.
